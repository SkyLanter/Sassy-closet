## Locked conservative method

1. **Billable kg** = `max(actual kg, L×W×H cm / 6000)`, then **round up 0.5 kg**. Re-fetched 2026-09-10 from [calculator.cainiao.us](https://calculator.cainiao.us/) `static/js/calculator.js` (`Last-Modified: Sun, 13 Oct 2024 21:00:57 GMT`): `finalWeight = Math.max(volumeWeight, weight)` with `volumeWeight = L*W*H/6000`, then `Math.ceil(weight * 2) / 2`. Same rule as [Cainiao 101](https://www.cainiao.us/p/cainiao101) (page 2024-09-25 / stamp 2026-03-31; accessed 2026-09-10).
2. **Live App anchor** (2026-09-09, first-party checkout): **2.547 kg** billable → sea tax-incl **¥158+** · air tax-incl **¥375+**. Cite: Boss App; [`docs/china-us-best-ship-rate-20260909.md`](./china-us-best-ship-rate-20260909.md) @ `5886c4eb07f9d414c4b08af0be6605a5a5cd9ca3`.
3. **App-linear (illustrative — still one live point):**  
   `¥62.03/kg sea` = `158 / 2.547`  
   `¥147.23/kg air` ≈ **¥147/kg** = `375 / 2.547`  
   `total = billable_kg × ¥/kg` (full precision, then display).  
   **Label: illustrative.** Not a published unit card. **STALE 2025 marketing cards must not undercut this.**
4. **Soft 11-mã haul (default):** **4.0 kg** billable — scout **no-good-merge / sum-chargeable** (drinkware-adjusted 合单), **not** the 3.0 kg optimistic merge. Show **5.0 kg** as the next sensitivity.
5. **Share (default):** **weight-weighted** by letter grams so A / S / K take more of the box than tiny P / H. Grams model unchanged: A **360** · S **450** · K **650** · P **80** · H **40** (2,620 g). Drinkware heaviness is priced in the **4.0 kg box**, not by stealing allocation from clothes.
6. **Per-mã floor:** inbound sea never below the **0.5 kg sea step** (¥31.02 / **$4.61**). Inbound air never below the **0.5 kg air step** (¥73.62 / **$10.95**). Binds P / H on both 4 kg and 5 kg sheets.
7. **Default MARGIN column = sea.** Air tax-incl is the expensive speed alternative. Product 50% GM floors stay on **sea** unless Boss says air.
8. **Customer US flat ship = TBD.** Separate column only. Not in product sell.

**Do not** freeze ¥158 or $2.52. **Do not** use 2025 ¥47 / ¥57 / first ¥52.35 cards as checkout or as the margin inbound.

`actual = (Σ item g + 80 + 15N) / 1000`  
`vol = (Σ packed cm³ × 1.15) / 6000`  
`bill = ceil_half(max(actual, vol))`
