# Message-first trust

## What strong shops do

Edelman’s 2025 Trust Barometer (cited in Shopify’s 2026 social-commerce brief) finds **88% of customers treat trust as equal to price and quality** ([Social commerce trends 2026](https://www.shopify.com/enterprise/blog/social-commerce-trends)). Meta pulled native in-app checkout (Sept 2025); Facebook/Instagram Shops are galleries that send people *out* to a brand surface. The remaining job of the site is to explain **what happens after they tap Message**.

Messenger-trust writing is consistent: respond as a real Page, say who you are, do not surprise people with fees ([Build trust through Messenger](https://advertiserreview.com/social-media/messenger/trust-signals/)). Sprout still ranks Facebook as the #1 discovery + service network ([Social commerce](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/social-commerce/)).

For this closet that means:

- Message is the checkout. Hold / “Nhắn tin hỏi giá” is merchandising, not a bug.
- Never invent a ship $ or a card total. Shopify’s agent-buy post-mortem shows “displayed price ≠ final price” as a top failure ([Perplexity bought from our store](https://idukki.io/blog/perplexity-bought-from-our-store)).
- Do not add Square Save or in-chat Facebook Send. The Page URL is the only public identity. No personal name.

## What we already had

- Messenger CTAs, ship-draft (address + mã), Zelle as a method word, Hold first-class.

## Applied here

- PDP / footer / home ribbon: one how-to line (*Inbox for ship quote or cash meetup*). Full Inquiry → Hold → Zelle → Taobao steps on `/how-to-buy`. Message CTAs are `m.me?text=` with the mã. Hold is photo-check, not Reserved.

## Deliberately not applied

- Invented return windows, warehouse SLAs, or card checkout “to look finished”.
